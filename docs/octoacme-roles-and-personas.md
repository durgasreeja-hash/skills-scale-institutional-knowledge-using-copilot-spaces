# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsor / Business Owner

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors or Business Owners provide strategic sponsorship, funding, and accountability for desired business outcomes. They help set direction, approve trade-offs, and ensure the project remains aligned to business priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm business value, strategic fit, and funding priorities
- Approve major scope, timeline, or resource trade-offs
- Participate in key go/no-go decisions and milestone reviews
- Sponsor cross-functional alignment and escalation when business priorities change
- Support the Product Manager and Project Manager in resolving competing priorities

### Goals
- Maximize return on investment and strategic alignment
- Keep the initiative focused on business outcomes
- Reduce delays caused by unclear priorities or unaligned stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Steering updates and milestone reviews
- Decision checkpoints and escalation discussions
- Business outcomes and risk summaries for leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with the Product Manager on outcome definition and trade-offs
- Partners with the Project Manager on timeline, scope, and escalation decisions
- Reviews major risks or blockers with project leadership and stakeholders
- Aligns with engineering and QA leadership when resourcing or business constraints affect delivery

---

## Engineering / Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering or Technical Leads guide technical direction, delivery quality, and engineering execution. They translate product goals into feasible solutions and help the team manage technical dependencies and risks.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical design direction and architecture constraints
- Estimate effort and sequence work with engineering teams
- Coordinate technical dependencies, integration planning, and design reviews
- Support developers in delivering maintainable, testable solutions
- Escalate delivery risks or technical debt that could affect scope or quality

### Goals
- Deliver robust, scalable, and sustainable solutions
- Balance delivery speed with engineering quality and maintainability
- Surface technical risks early and propose mitigations

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Delivery risks and dependency updates during standups and planning
- Technical recommendations during sprint and release readiness reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to align technical feasibility with user needs
- Coordinates with Project Managers on sequencing, dependencies, and delivery risk
- Guides Developers in implementation quality and engineering standards
- Collaborates with QA/Testing on testability, defect triage, and release readiness

---

## UX / Product Designer

### Role Summary
UX or Product Designers shape the user experience and ensure the solution remains usable, accessible, and aligned with customer needs. They bridge customer insight and interaction design with delivery teams.

### Responsibilities
- Define user journeys, workflows, and interaction patterns
- Conduct or support user research, usability testing, and feedback synthesis
- Turn product goals into user-centered design decisions
- Validate that solutions meet usability and accessibility expectations
- Partner with Product Managers and Developers on trade-offs and iteration

### Goals
- Improve product usability and customer satisfaction
- Reduce friction in key user workflows
- Ensure design quality is considered during planning and delivery

### Typical Communication
- User research summaries and design reviews
- Feedback loops with Product and delivery teams
- UX validation and acceptance discussions before release

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to refine problem framing and feature priorities
- Collaborates with Developers to translate designs into feasible implementation
- Provides input to QA/Testing for usability and accessibility validation
- Keeps Project Managers informed of design-related dependencies or schedule impacts

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data or Analytics Leads help define how success is measured and how product changes are instrumented for learning. They ensure the team can evaluate outcomes and diagnose product performance.

### Responsibilities
- Define measurement strategy, KPIs, and instrumentation requirements
- Validate that dashboards, event tracking, and reporting support the project goals
- Partner with Product Managers on outcome measurement and success criteria
- Help interpret metrics during review cycles and after release
- Identify data quality or analytics gaps that could affect decision-making

### Goals
- Make product impact measurable and actionable
- Support data-informed decision making throughout the lifecycle
- Improve confidence in adoption, performance, and customer value

### Typical Communication
- KPI definitions and reporting reviews
- Metrics analysis and experiment summaries
- Release-impact and success metric check-ins

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to define outcomes and track success metrics
- Coordinates with Developers and QA/Testing on instrumentation and validation
- Provides context to Project Managers for milestone and release health reviews
- Helps Sponsors understand measurable progress and business impact

---

## Security / Privacy Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads ensure that projects meet required safety, compliance, and risk standards. They help identify controls, review escalation paths, and support risk reduction before release.

### Responsibilities
- Assess security, privacy, and compliance requirements for the initiative
- Review architecture, data handling, and access patterns for gaps
- Recommend mitigations and control requirements for rollout or production use
- Coordinate with engineering and QA teams on secure implementation and testing
- Escalate high-risk issues through the appropriate project or leadership channels

### Goals
- Reduce product and data risk before and during release
- Support compliant, trustworthy delivery
- Ensure security and privacy concerns are visible early and addressed in a timely manner

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints and risk assessments
- Privacy and compliance issue tracking
- Escalation updates for high-impact concerns

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers and Technical Leads on review and remediation planning
- Supports QA/Testing with validation of security and privacy-related controls
- Works with Project Managers on risk tracking and escalation paths
- Provides input to Sponsors and Product Managers when business or compliance trade-offs are needed

---

## Operations / Support Representative

### Role Summary
Operations or Support Representatives help ensure the solution is ready for real-world use, support, and ongoing service reliability. They provide practical insight into operational readiness, support burden, and customer impact.

### Responsibilities
- Review operational readiness, onboarding, and support workflows
- Identify support impacts, customer-facing dependencies, and training needs
- Coordinate release handoff, monitoring, and incident communication requirements
- Share lessons from support or operations experience to improve planning and rollout
- Help evaluate whether the solution is sustainable to run and support after launch

### Goals
- Reduce operational disruption and support escalations
- Improve service reliability and customer experience after release
- Ensure dependencies for support and operations are addressed before launch

### Typical Communication
- Readiness reviews and release handoff meetings
- Support-impact and incident communication updates
- Post-release feedback and operational lessons learned

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinates with Project Managers on deployment readiness and release planning
- Provides release feedback to Product Managers and Sponsors on customer experience and support impact
- Works with Developers and QA/Testing to validate support scenarios and operational workflows
- Helps the team monitor whether a solution remains manageable in production

---

## Role Interaction Summary

These roles are intended to improve accountability and clarity across the project lifecycle. In smaller teams, one person may cover multiple responsibilities, but the underlying ownership and communication patterns should still be explicit. The Project Manager and Product Manager remain central to scheduling, prioritization, and cross-team coordination, while additional roles provide specialized guidance in sponsorship, technical delivery, design, data, security, and operations.
